## Cross-unit coherence

- **Vectors:** U1 establishes components and vector operations; U2–U3 apply them
  to forces, U5 to linear momentum, and U6 to torque and angular momentum.
- **Circular motion:** U1 treats particle kinematics, U3 radial dynamics, and U6
  rigid-body rotation. The rigid relation `v=rω` is not generalized to arbitrary
  circular motion.
- **Gravity:** U2–U3 use local weight, U4 states `mgy`/`mgh` under a uniform-field
  approximation, and U7 uses `g(r)=GM/r²` and `U=-GMm/r` with reference at infinity.
- **Energy:** U4 defines work, potentials, system boundaries and conservation;
  U6 adds rotational kinetic energy/work/power; U7 distinguishes orbital and SHM
  energy with their conditions and signs.
- **Momentum and systems:** “momento lineal” and “momento angular” remain explicit.
  U4 energy, U5 center-of-mass/external impulse, and U6 translation-plus-rotation
  state their system boundaries.
- **Spring:** U4 elastic potential and U7 restoring force/SHM use displacement
  from equilibrium and a consistent zero of potential.
- **Friction:** U3 distinguishes static and kinetic regimes, U4 treats transfer to
  internal energy, U6 treats rolling static friction, and Pulley Lab preserves the
  static transition. Static friction is not stated as always equal to `μ_sN`.
- **Approximations:** constant acceleration, ideal strings/pulleys, uniform field,
  fixed/principal axes, no-slip rolling, `m≪M`, small-angle pendulum, underdamped
  response, slow precession, and Schwarzschild's GR context are visible where used.

The notation audit found no incompatible same-page reuse or incorrect units:
torque uses N·m, angular momentum uses kg·m²/s, and impulse/momentum equivalence is
presented as N·s = kg·m/s.
